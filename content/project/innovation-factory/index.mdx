#### Project Overview

Built a **corporate marketing website** on **WordPress** for KPMG’s Innovation Factory, supplier of the PIT® innovation platform used by global enterprises; implementing a custom theme based on Genesis framework with **fully responsive** templates and a lightweight component system.

#### Scope & Contributions

- **Frontend implementation** — Translated design mockups and UX flows into accessible, responsive HTML/CSS/JS with reusable sections (hero, in-page nav, parallax blocks, sliders).
- **Theme development (Genesis)** — Developed a child theme with clean templates, partials, and sensible customization hooks.
- **Custom plugin** — Added content types and admin screens to manage blog posts, slideshows, partners, clients, team members, contacts, and custom landing pages.
- **Editor experience** — Structured fields and page builders so non-technical editors could publish without developer help.
- **Performance & SEO** — Image optimization and lazy-loading, asset minification, meta/Open Graph tags, and sitemap support.
- **Email template** — Designed and built a **responsive Mailchimp newsletter template** matching the site’s look-and-feel for consistent brand communications.
- **CI/CD** — Configured **Jenkins** jobs for repeatable builds and deployments.

#### Outcomes

- **Consistent brand presence** across web and email campaigns.
- **Faster publishing** for editors via purpose-built content models and admin UX.
- **Stable releases** and easier maintenance through a clear theme/plugin split and Jenkins-based deployment.
